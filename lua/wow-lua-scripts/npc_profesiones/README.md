# NPC Professions

The NPC's job is basically to check that the player is level 80, and then raise their profession skills up to 450. What the player must do is go to the profession instructor (to prevent a player from having more than 2 professions, because if not, we should control how many professions they have), and once the profession level is less than 450, the NPC is in charge of raising it to 450, but without teaching them recipes.
